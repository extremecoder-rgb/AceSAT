# AceSAT Education AI-Agent — Project Write-Up

## 1. The Problem: The SAT Prep Inequality

The SAT is a critical gatekeeper for higher education. However, the preparation ecosystem is highly unequal. Wealthier students access high-end, adaptive tutoring services costing thousands of dollars, while students in underserved communities rely on static, one-size-fits-all study guides or dry textbooks. Traditional online platforms offer question banks but lack active, intelligent steering—failing to diagnose specific foundational gaps, build personalized paths, or dynamically adjust explanation depth.

## 2. Our Solution: AceSAT Adaptive AI-Agent

AceSAT is an **AI education agent** (not a chatbot) built to democratize high-end tutoring. Designed with a high-contrast, distraction-free **Neobrutalist UI**, the app serves as an active educational partner for the student.

### How the Agent Works (End-to-End Loop):

1. **Diagnosis**: Upon onboarding, the student completes a targeted diagnostic quiz. Rather than just calculating a percentage score, the agent analyzes the responses to identify specific topic vulnerabilities (e.g., *Inference* in Reading or *Quadratic Factoring* in Math) and establishes baseline proficiencies.
2. **Personalized Study Plan**: Using the `nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b` model (accessed via a secure backend proxy), the agent constructs a personalized, ordered 4-step study plan tailored to the student's weakest areas, estimating time commitments for each step.
3. **Active Steering & Adaptation**: In the practice room, the agent takes full control of the curriculum:
   - It selects the target category based on the student's lowest proficiency.
   - It dynamically adapts the difficulty level of the next question (Easy, Medium, Hard) depending on whether the last answer was correct or incorrect.
   - It records attempts locally in a Room database, serving as long-term memory.
   - It provides structured, step-by-step reasoning explanations generated by the NIM model to ensure deep learning.

## 3. The Impact: Democratizing Elite Tutoring

AceSAT turns any mobile device into a dedicated 1-on-1 tutor. By actively managing the learning path rather than placing the burden of selection on the student, it keeps students in their optimal learning zone (neither bored by easy questions nor discouraged by impossibly hard ones). In underserved schools, this agent acts as an force multiplier for teachers, allowing students to learn at their own pace with world-class feedback, closing the preparation gap one question at a time.
